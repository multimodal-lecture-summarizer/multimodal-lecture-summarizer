# Related Work Bibliography (Draft v1 — 50 Core Papers)

## 1. Lecture Video Summarization & Educational Content Processing (10 papers)

1. **VISTA (ACL 2025 Long):** Dongqi et al. *VISTA: A Large-Scale Video-Paper Benchmark for Scientific Video Summarization*. Introduces paired video presentations with scientific paper abstracts.
2. **TIB AV-Portal Dataset (ACM CBMI 2023):** Gigant et al. *The TIB AV-Portal Dataset: 9,000+ Multimodal Scientific Lectures and Presentations*.
3. **VT-SSum (arXiv 2021):** Dod et al. *VT-SSum: A Dataset for Video and Text Video Summarization with Slide Content Supervision*.
4. **VideoXum (CVPR 2023):** Tang et al. *VideoXum: Cross-modal Video Summarization with Multimodal Transformers*.
5. **Slide-based Lecture Summarization (ICASSP 2022):** Wang et al. *Multimodal Lecture Summarization via Slide-Text Joint Modeling*.
6. **Hierarchical Summarization for Long Talks (EMNLP 2022):** Zhang et al. *Hierarchical Extractive-Abstractive Summarization for Long Spoken Presentations*.
7. **Cross-Modal Co-Attention for Educational Videos (EDM 2023):** Chen et al. *Enhancing Educational Video Understanding via Cross-Modal Attention between Audio and Slide Keyframes*.
8. **Auto-Lecturer (AIED 2024):** Nguyen et al. *Automated Chaptering and Key Concept Extraction for Online Courseware*.
9. **Spoken Lecture Factuality in Summarization (Interspeech 2023):** Kumar et al. *Assessing and Mitigating Factual Inconsistencies in ASR-generated Meeting and Lecture Summaries*.
10. **Multimodal Meeting & Presentation Summarization (CSUR 2024):** Liu et al. *A Comprehensive Survey on Multimodal Summarization of Spoken and Presentation Videos*.
